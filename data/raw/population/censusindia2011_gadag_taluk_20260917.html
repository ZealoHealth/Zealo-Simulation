Gadag Taluk Population - Gadag, Karnataka

Gadag is a Taluk located in Gadag district of Karnataka. It is one of 5 Taluks of Gadag district. There are 64 villages and 2 towns in Gadag Taluk.

As per the Census India 2011, Gadag Taluk has 77,639 households, population of 367,258 of which 184,572 are males and 182,686 are females. The population of children between age 0-6 is 43,497 which is 11.84% of total population.

The sex-ratio of Gadag Taluk is around 990 compared to 973 which is average of Karnataka state. The literacy rate of Gadag Taluk is 69.54% out of which 76.35% males are literate and 62.66% females are literate. The total area of Gadag is 1,092 sq.km with population density of 336 per sq.km.

Out of total population, 47.89% of population lives in Urban area and 52.11% lives in Rural area. There are 14.19% Scheduled Caste (SC) and 4.8% Scheduled Tribe (ST) of total population in Gadag Taluk.
